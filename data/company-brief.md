# Juniper Workshop Lab — fictional company brief

This company, its product and all claims below are fictional teaching material.

## Offer and audience

Juniper Workshop Lab helps small service teams turn repeated manual tasks into simple, understandable tools. The audience is team leads at organisations with 5–50 employees. The demo product is **ClearDesk**, a guided workflow board for recurring work.

The main action is “Plan a demo.” In a workshop prototype, this action collects a name and work email locally and displays “Demo request prepared — nothing has been sent.” Do not connect a real inbox by default.

## Approved fictional copy

- Headline: Make space for the work that matters.
- Product description: ClearDesk keeps a task's inputs, steps and handover in one place.
- Features: reusable checklists, clear ownership, visible exceptions.
- Demo price: EUR 40 per team per month. This is fictional example pricing, not a real offer.
- Contact: hello@juniper-workshop.invalid.
- Do not add invented customers, ratings, savings percentages or certifications.

## Voice and visual direction

Warm, clear and practical. Use short sentences and explain unfamiliar terms. Say “try a workflow” rather than promising full automation. Use dark green, warm cream and one orange accent. Use accessible contrast and readable body type. No logo asset is supplied; a text wordmark is sufficient.

## Shared prototype story

A team receives weekly requests through three different channels. They copy each request into a table, ask for missing details and assign an owner. ClearDesk's first prototype imports a CSV, flags missing information and produces a review list. A human decides what to send or accept.

The site, deck and media prototype may reuse this story. Always show “Fictional workshop company” somewhere visible.
