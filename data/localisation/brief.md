# Localisation brief

Fictional company: Juniper Workshop Lab. Product: ClearDesk.

Source locale: en-GB. Default exercise target: de-DE. The participant may choose another locale and adapt the glossary explicitly.

Audience: small service-team leads. Voice: warm, direct and practical; use polite “Sie” in German. Translate meaning naturally; do not add promises or remove uncertainty. Keep “ClearDesk” unchanged.

The JSON uses stable flat keys. Variables must remain exactly as written. Dates and money are supplied by the application, so translate the surrounding sentence without hardcoding a particular date or currency. Test German date and currency formatting separately using 2026-10-22 and EUR 2500.00. The source fixture does not define a live billing system.

Preview in a narrow phone viewport and allow buttons to wrap or expand. Deliver the target JSON plus a terminology/QA report. No ISO certification claim is part of this exercise.
