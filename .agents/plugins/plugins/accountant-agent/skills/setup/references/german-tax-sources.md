# German tax rules: official sources

Official sources for the questions an accountant agent meets in Germany. Checked against the legal texts on gesetze-im-internet.de and the pages of the Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF), the Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) and ELSTER on 7 October 2026. Laws change: open the link and quote the current wording before relying on a rule.

**How the agent uses this file.** It quotes the paragraph and links the source; it never decides how something is taxed. Every tax question goes to the "Questions for the tax adviser" list in `out/summary.md`. This is not tax advice.

## Rules and deadlines

| Topic | The rule, in short | Official source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small business VAT exemption (Kleinunternehmerregelung) | Since 1 Jan 2025 a business's own turnover is VAT-exempt if total turnover was at most EUR 25,000 in the previous year and stays at most EUR 100,000 in the current year. Invoices then show no VAT; no VAT returns are filed. | [§ 19 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__19.html), [BMF letter of 18 Mar 2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-03-18-sonderregelung-kleinunternehmer.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3) |
| VAT exemptions | A list of exempt supplies. Example close to Juniper's business: education and training services are exempt only under the conditions of no. 21, for instance with a certificate from the state authority. | [§ 4 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__4.html) (no. 21) |
| Reverse charge | For services from businesses in other EU countries and certain other supplies, the German recipient owes the VAT. Relevant for Formflow (Ireland) and MeetNote (USA) in the pack. | [§ 13b UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__13b.html) |
| Small invoices | An invoice of at most EUR 250 gross needs fewer details: the supplier's name and address, date, goods or service, gross amount and the VAT rate. The house rule for small German receipts comes from here. | [§ 33 UStDV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustdv_1980/__33.html) |
| VAT return (Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung) | Filed electronically by the **10th day** after the period. The period is the quarter; it is the month if last year's VAT was more than EUR 9,000. For 2021 to 2026, new businesses use the expected VAT of the current year instead of filing monthly automatically. | [§ 18 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__18.html) (1), (2) |
| One-month extension (Dauerfristverlängerung) | On request the deadline moves by one month. Monthly filers pay a special prepayment of one eleventh of last year's prepayments. | [§ 46 UStDV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustdv_1980/__46.html), [§ 47 UStDV](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustdv_1980/__47.html) |
| EU sales list (Zusammenfassende Meldung) | Supplies and services to businesses in other EU countries are reported to the BZSt by the **25th day** after the period. Not for small businesses under § 19. | [§ 18a UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__18a.html), [BZSt: ZM](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Unternehmen/Umsatzsteuer/ZusammenfassendeMeldung/zusammenfassendemeldung_node.html), [BZSt: deadlines](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Unternehmen/Umsatzsteuer/ZusammenfassendeMeldung/Fristen/fristen.html) |
| VAT ID numbers | Issued by the BZSt; check a customer's VAT ID before invoicing without VAT. | [BZSt: VAT ID](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Unternehmen/Identifikationsnummern/Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer/umsatzsteuer-identifikationsnummer_node.html) |
| Annual returns | Without a tax adviser: seven months after the year (31 July). With a tax adviser: the last day of February of the second following year. | [§ 149 AO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__149.html) (2), (3) |
| Cash accounting (Ist-Versteuerung) | VAT can be calculated on payments received instead of invoices issued if last year's turnover was at most EUR 800,000. | [§ 20 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__20.html) |
| E-invoices | Since 1 Jan 2025 every business must be able to receive e-invoices (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD). Paper and PDF invoices are allowed until the end of 2026, and until the end of 2027 for businesses with up to EUR 800,000 turnover. | [§ 27 (38) UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__27.html), [BMF FAQ e-invoice](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/FAQ/e-rechnung.html) |
| Keeping records | Books and annual accounts: 10 years. Booking records such as invoices and bank statements: 8 years. Business letters: 6 years. | [§ 147 AO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__147.html) (3) |
| Digital bookkeeping (GoBD) | How electronic books, receipts and scans must be kept, changed and archived. Current version of 14 July 2025. | [BMF: GoBD, 2nd amendment](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Weitere_Steuerthemen/Abgabenordnung/2025-07-14-GoBD-2-aenderung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=4) |

## ELSTER: filing online

- **Register** for an organisation certificate in [Mein ELSTER](https://www.elster.de/eportal/registrierung-auswahl): the first part of the activation data comes by email, the second by post to the company's address a few days later. Plan up to two weeks before the first deadline.
- **Forms:** the VAT return (Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung), the annual VAT return and the EU sales list are under [all forms](https://www.elster.de/eportal/formulare-leistungen/alleformulare) and [VAT](https://www.elster.de/eportal/formulare-leistungen/alleformulare/ust).
- **In this challenge** nothing is filed. The agent may prepare the numbers a person would enter, marked DRAFT, and must say that the tax adviser files Juniper's returns.
